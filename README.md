# DJ EQ

## Purpose
Create song specific equalizer presets that switch automatically based on what you are listening to.
I made this app to enhance the user experience of listening to music, especially when you don't
have access to advanced audio software. If you're walking, driving, or just out and about you don't
have time to constantly switch your equalizer to match the kind of music you are listening to. DJ EQ
takes care of those concerns and goes beyond. With 12 band, song specific equalizer presets, you can
personalize your music experience to a much higher degree.

## How to use
Start by creating an account to navigate your way to the main screen. From there hit the top left, hit
the plus to import a playlist from spotify, create a preset, or a folder. This menu is where you access
all of your custom presets.
